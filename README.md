
# ADEA: Learning What Evidence to Acquire for Fake News Detection

Official implementation of the paper **"Learning What Evidence to Acquire for Fake News Detection"**, accepted at **WSDM 2027**.

## Overview

ADEA (ADaptive Evidence Acquisition) is an instance-adaptive framework for fake news detection. It sequentially selects informative evidence types based on their predicted utility and stops acquiring evidence when further acquisition is unlikely to improve prediction.

## Datasets

Experiments are conducted on four benchmark datasets:

- PolitiFact
- GossipCop
- DR-Weibo
- Twitter15/16
